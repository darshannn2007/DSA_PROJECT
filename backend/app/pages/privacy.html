Privacy & Cookie Notice

Last updated: 25 November 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect only your username, optional campus email, profile details, skills, connections, posts, and uploaded images. Passwords are stored exclusively as Argon2id hashes.

2. Essential Cookies

Authentication uses an essential HttpOnly session cookie and a CSRF protection cookie. Optional analytics/attribution is disabled unless you grant explicit consent.

3. Export & Deletion

You can export your account data as JSON or delete your account at any time from Edit My Profile.
