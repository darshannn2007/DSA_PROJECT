Common Questions

How are connection degrees and shortest paths calculated?

CampusHive models accepted connections as an undirected graph in Python using adjacency lists and runs Breadth-First Search (BFS) to find the shortest hop chain (1st, 2nd, 3rd degree).

How does People You May Know work?

We look at two-hop neighbors in the social graph and rank candidates using Jaccard similarity on shared connections, excluding existing connections, pending requests, and blocked users.

Who can see my profile and posts?

You can set your profile visibility to Campus (all active students) or Connections Only in Edit My Profile. Blocked users cannot view your profile, posts, or media.

How do I report abuse or delete my account?

Use the Report or Block buttons on any profile or post. You can export your data as JSON or permanently delete your account from Edit My Profile.
